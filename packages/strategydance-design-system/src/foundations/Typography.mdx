# Typography

Oswald, at 400 only, sets the titles `h1` to `h3` in secondary, tracked tight: `font-display`,
also reachable as `font-heading`. Inter sets everything else: 400 for body, 500 for labels, 600
for emphasis, through `font-sans`. Sizes are Tailwind's `text-*` scale.

## Display

<Typeset
  fontFamily="'Oswald Variable', 'Arial Narrow', sans-serif"
  fontWeight={400}
  fontSizes={['1.25rem', '1.5rem', '1.875rem', '2.25rem', '3rem', '3.75rem']}
  sampleText="Build the company, not the busywork"

Set as tight as its headlines are, Oswald's descenders hang below its lines. Where a text in it
hides what overflows it, as a clamp does, they are cut off unless it takes `descender-room`, which
keeps the room they need at any line height without moving the layout.
