# Colors

Mostly neutral, with primary for actions. Primary `#0051A3` is the action and brand color.
Focus is secondary: a 2px outline around controls, and the border of a field. Secondary
`#142A41` is for headings, dark surfaces and inverse areas. Both scales were derived around
those two anchors. Neutrals and the status colors are Tailwind's own.
