# Typography

Oswald, at 400 only, sets the titles `h1` to `h3` in secondary, tracked tight: `font-display`,
also reachable as `font-heading`. Inter sets everything else: 400 for body, 500 for labels, 600
for emphasis, through `font-sans`. Sizes are Tailwind's `text-*` scale.
